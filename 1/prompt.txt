review draft for video script:
Storm Fund is a financial resilience engine visualized as a weather station. Simulate shock events to see how they impact an entire population or analyze individual accounts and see their runway forecast.

Before any shocks, 1% of accounts are already in a storm warning or critical stage, which means they have under 3 months of runway. If a major shock event like a job loss impacted every household, 10% of accounts would have under 3 months of runway. This can have a catastropic effect on the country. Long term, this turns into debt, evicton risk, and delayed healthcare for 450 households in this dataset along. They spend less at local shops and lose bargaining power over wages. When shock events are common, the defaults cluster and banks tighten credit for everyone.  

The cheapest intervention is to build plans to move an account from storm warning back to at least 6 months runway. Take any account, see their financial forecast, and watch what a shock does to it. From here we calculate the required cushion to withstand shock events. We can see how much actions such as deferring loan payments and reduced discretionary spending can save us.
