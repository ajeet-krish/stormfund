Resilience Weather Station: Judge Sheet

Sheridan Datathon 2026, Tangerine track Resilience Rewired. One page of definitions, arithmetic, keys, and the rehearsal script, so the demo runs with the site down.

Synthetic data: fixture accounts only, no real customers

Press Control + P to print this sheet on one US Letter page.

The six numbers

Number Definition from monthly_summary.csv
I money in Median of money_in
F fixed costs Median of household_bills + insurance + loan_payments (shown, not used in runway)
S spending Median of money_out
B buffer Mean of avg_daily_balance over the last 6 rows, sorted by month
H household bills Median of household_bills
P loan payment monthly_payment in loans.csv when the loan still runs at the end of 2025, else 0

Runway formula and rounding

buffer_left = B minus one off costs (one off costs subtract from B)

gap = spending after shock minus income after shock

  • buffer_left at or below 0: runway is 0, band Critical
  • gap at or below 0: runway reads no shortfall, 120 plus, band Clear
  • otherwise: runway = min(buffer_left divided by gap, 120 months)

Runway prints rounded to 1 decimal. Internally the engine keeps integer cents and integer tenths of a month, so two surfaces can never disagree by a cent.

Worked example: account 68257

I 650326c ($6,503.26), S 716084c ($7,160.84), B 643785c ($6,437.85)

H 430672c ($4,306.72), P 284493c ($2,844.93)

gap = S minus I = 716084 minus 650326 = 65758c ($657.58)

Baseline: deci 98, so 9.8 months, band Fair skies

Layoff: income after = 0.55 x I = 357679c ($3,576.79)

gap after = 716084 minus 357679 = 358405c ($3,584.05)

Runway = 643785 divided by 358405 = 1.8 months (deci 18)

Fair skies becomes Storm warning. The 6 month EI horizon is display only.

Trace: scripts/engine/golden_fixtures.json, account 68257.

The five verdict bands

Band Threshold on the shocked runway
Critical Buffer gone, or under 1.0 month
Storm warning 1.0 to 2.9 months
Watch 3.0 to 5.9 months
Fair skies 6.0 to 11.9 months
Clear 12.0 months and up, or no shortfall

Twist card arithmetic

Card What the engine changes
Laid off income after = 0.55 x I (EI), shown over a 6 month horizon
Bills up 15% spending after = S + 0.15 x H
Furnace repair one off cost = 1.0 x S, taken out of the buffer before the runway
Laid off and bills up 0.55 x I and S + 0.15 x H together, 6 month horizon

The duration shown on a card is display only: it never enters the math, it only sets the horizon the shocked runway is judged against.

Guard lines (advice, never a block)

  • unknown id: Account id is not in the loaded profile set
  • pension income: Pension income account: money in is pension, not wages
  • pension vs layoff: Layoff twist simulated against pension income: this account has no wage to lose, read the verdict as a stress test
  • zero income: Zero income: money in is 0, so the layoff and EI framing does not apply
  • thin history: Thin history: fewer than 18 monthly rows, so the baseline medians rest on a short window
  • negative buffer: Savings buffer is below zero: the baseline already reads 0.0 months of runway
  • defaulted loan: Loan in arrears (status D): the account still pays a loan it is behind on. Loan defaulted and closed (status B): no payment runs, so the loan levers do not apply
  • no loan: No running loan payment: the loan deferral lever does not apply
  • zero bills: Household bills are 0: a bills shock cannot move this account

Any known account id stays selectable whatever its flags say. Guards inform, they never gate.

Keys

Key Action
1 Play the Laid off card
2 Play the Bills up 15% card
3 Play the Furnace repair card
4 Play the Laid off and bills up card
r Reset the twist, keep the account
Shift + R Reset everything, account included
/ Focus the account lookup
Esc Layered dismiss: open typed shock gate, then the lookup query, then everything
n Open a random safe demo account

Keys stay quiet while you type in a field, except Esc, which always works. Ctrl, Meta, and Alt never fire a judge key.

Rehearsal script (five steps, 2000 ms target)

  1. 68257, Laid off. Read the verdict aloud: this account carries no guard flags.
  2. 23838, Bills up 15%. Say: No running loan payment: the loan deferral lever does not apply.
  3. 98952, Laid off. Say: Layoff twist simulated against pension income: this account has no wage to lose, read the verdict as a stress test.
  4. 49900, Laid off and bills up. Say: Zero income: money in is 0, so the layoff and EI framing does not apply. Then note the thin history and the zero bills.
  5. 42988, Furnace repair. Say: Savings buffer is below zero: the baseline already reads 0.0 months of runway, and the loan is in arrears (status D).

Verdict on the stopwatch: fast under 2000 ms, ok under 4000 ms, slow otherwise. The in app rehearsal dock runs the same five steps.
