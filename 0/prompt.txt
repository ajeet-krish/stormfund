I built Storm Fund, a financial resilience engine visualized as a weather forecast. Pulling from an anonymized bank dataset (4,500 accounts, 1.06 million transactions from Tangerine), the tool is designed for anyone to understand their current financial situation. Simulate a shock event (job loss with EI at 55 percent, bills up 15 percent, a furnace repair) and the forecast changes live, showing you how much of a buffer you have in the case of an emergency. Using this data, we can build an emergency fund plan to better prepare for a rainy day.
